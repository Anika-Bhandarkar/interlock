From a guess to a placement

Strongest-pair mode is a separate experiment: it commits the highest-scoring pair, then grows outward by averaging raw matching scores against all touching neighbors. There is no rectangular boundary, refinement stage, or supplied corner. Its location metric uses one global translation to compare the final shape with the reference.

Stage 2 proposes a piece for each slot, visiting the most confident next slot first. Muted pieces are provisional and may appear in several slots. Stage 3 revisits the board, swaps guesses, and commits confident placements. Stage 4 assigns remaining pieces without duplicates.

The timeline shows actual solver snapshots, not the model’s training process or wall-clock inference time. Metrics describe the final board, regardless of the selected playback step. The reference is shown for inspection; only anchored mode supplies its top-left piece to the solver.
